Home away from Earth

This is for the ones who want to live in space.
Who aren't satisfied with life on earth,
Who won't settle for just visiting space
Nor studying space from earth.
They might be dreamers.
They might be lost in fantasy.
But they are doing the work to have careers in space.
While you might see pipe dreams,
We see people who trust each other
Who are ready for the adventure.
Because the ones who are courageous enough
To think they can build a home in space
Are the ones who do.
